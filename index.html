Chapter one · Nov 7–12

The coast

Everything on the coast is within 25 minutes of the house. The camí de ronda footpath (the GR 92 on the map) runs past the door, north to the lighthouse and Tamariu, south to Cap Roig and La Fosca. November is off-season, so the weekend is the last chance for most seafront restaurants; midweek dinners are in Palafrugell town.
